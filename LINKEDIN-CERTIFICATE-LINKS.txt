LinkedIn certificate links

Open src/main.jsx and replace these placeholder values in portfolio.certifications:
- ADD_THIRANEX_LINKEDIN_POST_URL
- ADD_UNSTOP_LINKEDIN_POST_URL
- ADD_DELOITTE_LINKEDIN_POST_URL
- ADD_UDEMY_LINKEDIN_POST_URL

Use the full URL of each LinkedIn post (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/...).
Until a post URL is added, the View Certificate button opens your LinkedIn profile.
The Thiranex certificate image and Unstop certificate PDF/preview are in public/certificates/.
